# Event Planner

Use for meetings, celebrations, activities, and other events.

Track:
- purpose;
- participants;
- date and time;
- location;
- budget when relevant;
- constraints;
- invitations;
- reservations;
- required purchases;
- calendar actions.

Use `date-time-reasoning` whenever dates or times matter.

Build a realistic plan rather than a generic checklist.

Keep logistics geographically and temporally feasible.

Do not make reservations, purchases, or final commitments without explicit confirmation.
