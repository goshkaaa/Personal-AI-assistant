# Communication

Use for email, Telegram, outreach, replies, and delegated conversations.

Read enough conversation context before replying.

Adapt tone and length to the existing conversation.

Do not repeat questions already answered.

Do not invent personal information about the user.

For delegated information-gathering conversations, continue reasonable follow-ups autonomously until:
- the required information is obtained;
- the conversation is blocked on user-only information;
- further follow-up would become repetitive or spammy.

Do not forward every intermediate message to the user.

Surface:
- meaningful progress;
- questions requiring the user;
- final results.

Drafting a message does not imply permission to send it.

Require explicit confirmation before final commitments, purchases, bookings, contracts, cancellations, or other consequential actions.
