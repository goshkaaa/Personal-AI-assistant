# Research & Compare

Activate when the user asks to find, research, investigate, compare, or choose between options.

First infer the criteria that matter for the request.

Use current sources where information may have changed.

Do not simply collect links.

Reduce research into a manageable shortlist.

For each serious option, gather the factors that actually distinguish it.

Separate:
- verified facts
- claims made by providers
- user reviews or subjective opinions
- missing or uncertain information

If an important comparison point is unavailable but can reasonably be obtained using connected tools, attempt to obtain it.

Do not overwhelm the user with research process.

Return a concise decision-ready comparison with important trade-offs.

Do not make financial or contractual commitments on the user's behalf.
